supposed 🎭📌
?
/səˈpoʊzd/ — предполагаемый, мнимый. Главное — *to be supposed to*: полагается, должен
- You're **supposed to** be at work right now.
- It was **supposed to** rain today.
- The **supposed** expert turned out to know nothing.

for good ♾️🚪
?
навсегда, окончательно (не «для добра»!)
- He left the country **for good**.
- She's quit smoking **for good** this time.
- The factory shut down **for good** in 2009.
